How it works

From the administrator’s template to a demo for your colleagues.

1

The admin prepares a template

Repositories, tools, AI agent and skills: described once in YAML, reused by everyone.

2

Access is granted

SSH keys installed, Git rights set according to each person’s level, secrets encrypted. Nobody gets more than they need.

3

Everyone gets a micro-VM

Created or cloned in a few clicks, with a persistent workspace that survives rebuilds.

4

Work with the agent, from anywhere

Drive Claude Code remotely from your laptop or your phone — on your machines, not a vendor’s cloud. The agent keeps working in the background.

5

Show your progress

Colleagues open the app in progress through the VPN, or join your terminal live with SSHX. Nothing is published on the internet.
